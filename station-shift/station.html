Heron Station

The station everyone plays on, drawn from the same map file the game loads.

This station is weightless: crew float from tile to tile, holding the handrails along the walls.

  • Request console (in the Cargo Entrance): Anyone can request an item here. The request waits in the order console's list until Logistics approves or denies it, or whoever asked withdraws it. It shows what became of your requests: approved and on its way, landed on the pad, or denied. It shows no supply points.
  • Order console (in the Cargo Reception): For cargo: needs cargo access. Shows the station's supply points and the requests waiting. Approving one spends the item's cost and prints an order form here; denying takes it off the list. Shipping the pad here sends away the goods on the delivery pad for supply points.
  • Delivery pad (in the Cargo Storage Bay): Orders land here, marked for whoever asked until someone picks them up. Goods lying here can be shipped from the order console; the station's own things stay.
  • Filing cabinet (in the Cargo Reception): Where stamped order forms are filed: 2 supply points each.
  • Atmos console (in the Atmospherics): The atmos console. Lists every room with an air alarm, the worst first, with what is wrong; tap a room for its card. Any engineer can send a room Panic or Normal here, or Panic all red rooms (a 10 second cooldown). It flashes red on the map while a room's air is in trouble. It works only while its room has power.
  • Ration dispenser (in the Central Hub): A wall dispenser of ration packs: stand beside it and tap Take ration. It gives each person one pack every 4 minutes (18 percent of the hunger meter), whether or not there is a Chef, so no one starves for want of a cook. It works only while its room has power.
  • Air alarm (43, one in each room): A room's air alarm. Anyone can tap it to read the room's pressure, temperature and gases and the state of its air (OK, WARN, DANGER); only a station engineer's ID card changes its mode (Normal, Scrub, Panic, Fill, Cycle, Off). Its light on the map shows the state, dark without power. A room an engineer's card does not open (command, medical, science, security, cargo, the brig cells) has a second alarm in the maintenance tunnel beside its wall, so the air there can still be controlled.
  • Tunnel air alarms (22): the rooms an engineer's card does not open have an alarm in the maintenance tunnel beside their wall, so the air there can still be controlled.
  • Brig cells (3): A cell has one door to the brig corridor, with windows beside it. A Security Officer or the Captain, standing at the door with the card, can lock it while someone is inside, for a Station Law sentence of 2, 5, 10 minutes. Until the time runs out nobody opens it from either side: not with a card, a crowbar, a multitool or cut wires, and a power cut does not undo it (the lock is mechanical). The door shows the time left. A Security Officer or the Captain can release it early at the door. Once it is unlocked, whoever is inside leaves by it without a card.
