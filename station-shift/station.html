Heron Station

The station everyone plays on, drawn from the same map file the game loads.

GEN generator SUP supply console PAD delivery pad FIL filing cabinet MON crew monitor PWR power monitor AIR atmos alerts power panel cable table rack closet bench window solar panels; letters are items (see Items).

  • Supply console (in the Cargo Entrance): Shows the station's supply points. Anyone can request an item here; the request waits in its list until someone approves it, which spends the item's cost, or denies it, or whoever asked withdraws it. Shipping the pad here sends away the goods on the delivery pad for supply points.
  • Delivery pad (in the Cargo Storage Bay): Orders land here, marked for whoever asked until someone picks them up. Goods lying here can be shipped from the supply console; the station's own things stay.
  • Filing cabinet (in the Cargo Reception): Where stamped order forms are filed: 2 supply points each.
