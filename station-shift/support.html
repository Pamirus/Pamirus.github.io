Support

Something broken, confusing or unfair? Write to pamirfescioglu@gmail.com. Say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead; a screenshot helps.

Testing through TestFlight? You can also take a screenshot in the app and share it with TestFlight, or send feedback from the TestFlight app; both reach the developer.

Common problems

  • "Could not reach the server": the phone and the computer running the server must be on the same Wi-Fi, and the address must be the one the host gives you. The Connection check button on the first screen tests the connection.
  • "This app and the server are different versions": update the app in TestFlight; the host may need to update the server too.
  • Dropped out: open the app again within a few minutes and you are back in your body.
