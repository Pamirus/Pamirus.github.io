Privacy

Last updated 2026-11-24. This policy covers the Station Shift app, made by Zeki Pamir Fesçioğlu. Questions and requests: pamirfescioglu@gmail.com.

In short

Station Shift has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone. What you type into the game goes to the game server you join and to the other players in that game.

On your phone

The app remembers the name you play under, the last server address and the last job you picked, so you do not have to type them again. They stay on your phone and are removed when you delete the app.

When you play

The app connects to the game server whose address you enter. That server receives your name, what you do in the game and the lines you say, and passes them to the other players in the same game, who see your name and read your lines. The server does not store what you say.

For each finished shift the server keeps a short record: the names of the crew, their jobs, how many lines each said, what each repaired, and which incidents happened and how fast they were fixed. It is used to judge and improve the game, and it stays on the computer that runs the server. Ask at the address above and your entries are deleted.

Like any network connection, the server sees your IP address while you are connected; the game does not store it.

Practice alone runs a server inside the app on your phone; nothing leaves the phone.

TestFlight

If you test the app through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares with the developer the email address you were invited with, crash reports and any feedback you send, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms.

Children

The game has open chat between players and is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If the app starts to handle data differently, this page changes first and its date above with it.
